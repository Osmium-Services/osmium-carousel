**Osmium Carousel** manages the slides your site's design shows, usually on the homepage.

## Add a slide

1. Press **Add Slide**.
2. Enter a **Title** and, optionally, some **Text**.
3. Upload a **Picture** by dragging it in or clicking. JPG, PNG or WebP works, and the form tells you the minimum size.
4. Choose where it **Links to**: a **Page** on your site, or any **Address**. Add **Button text** if you want a button.
5. Tick **Turned on** and press **Save**.

## Control when and where it shows

- **Show from** and **Show until** are optional dates. The slide appears only between them.
- **Order** sets the position. A lower number shows first.
- **Group** lets one site have more than one carousel. Slides with the same group form one carousel. Leave it empty for the site's main carousel.
- **Exclusive** means that while the slide is live, only exclusive slides show. Use it for a one-off announcement.

## Change the slide size

On the Osmium Carousel page, set **Slide width** and **Slide height** and press **Save size**. A new picture is cropped from the centre to that size, and refused if it is smaller. Slides you already saved keep their old size.

## Turn off or remove a slide

Use **Turn off** to hide a slide without losing it, and **Turn on** to bring it back. **Delete** moves it to **Deleted Slides**, where **Purge All** removes them for good.
